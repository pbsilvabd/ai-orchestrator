## Objective and scope

Ordinary writes cannot reopen uncertain work or mutate accepted authority.

Out of scope: Adding a human override to generic updateTask or duplicating B14/B18.

## Implementation sequence

1. Preserve the ordinary edge table and immutable task/client/key/hash/request/acceptance fields.
2. Preserve attempt identity and start time; queued work with persisted dispatch cannot restart or claim unstarted cancellation.
3. Require coherent block/cancel/timestamp presence and freeze terminal lifecycle metadata while allowing partial artifacts.
4. Keep malformed legacy records fail-closed without rewriting; dispatch production, stop proof and human recovery remain separate cards.
